# Privacy Policy for Kitty Kaiten

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

Kitty Kaiten is a puzzle game for Android made by **Whirlwind Software**. This policy
describes what it does with your information.

## The short version

**Kitty Kaiten has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of
its own.** Your progress and settings stay on your phone, with a copy in your own Android
backup if you have backup turned on. The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**, and that is
the one place where information leaves your device for someone else.

## What Kitty Kaiten stores, and where

All of it is in the game's own private storage on your device, which other apps
cannot read. None of it is sent to us.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Your progress: levels won, stars, coins, your win streak, the pieces of your bar | The app's private storage |
| The cats you have met, their hearts and stories, and the Daily Feast streak and results | The app's private storage |
| Settings: music, sounds, vibration, motion, lighting | The app's private storage |
| A level left half-way, to go on with later | The app's private storage |
| How many full-screen ads have been shown, and whether ads were paid away | The app's private storage |

**Backup.** If Android backup is on for your phone, Android copies this game data to your own
Google account, encrypted, so your progress comes back on a new phone. That copy is
handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we cannot see it. You can turn backup off
in your phone's settings (**Settings → Google → Backup**).

Uninstalling the game deletes everything on the phone. A backup copy, if there is one, stays
in your Google account until Android replaces or removes it.

## Ads

Kitty Kaiten shows ads served by **Google AdMob**:

- **rewarded ads**, only when you choose to watch one (one more seat after the belt jams, or
  double coins after a win);
- an occasional **full-screen ad between levels**, never during one.

To show ads and count them, Google may collect your device's **advertising ID**, your
approximate location (from your IP address), and information about your device and how
you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive
this information ourselves, beyond totals of ads shown and earned.

## Your choices

If you are in the **EEA, the UK or Switzerland**, the game asks for your consent before
ads are personalised. If you are in a **US state with its own privacy law**, you can opt
out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Either way you can change your
choice at any time in the game: **Settings → Privacy choices**.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings
(**Settings → Google → Ads**), and see or change how Google uses your data in
[My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Purchases

If you buy something in the game (Remove Ads), the payment is handled entirely by
**Google Play** under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only the confirmation that the
purchase was made, never your payment details. "Restore" in Settings asks Google Play again
which purchases you own.

## Sharing and reviews

When you tap **Share** (for example a Daily Feast result), the game hands a short text to
your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. It contains your result or a link
to the game, nothing about you. If the game asks for a rating, that is Google Play's own
review sheet; your review goes to Google Play, not to us.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the
right to see, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to its use. We
keep no personal data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to hand over or
delete: what the game keeps is on your phone (and in your own backup) and goes when you
uninstall it and remove the backup. For the data Google collects for ads, use the choices
above. You can always write to us with a request.

## Children

Kitty Kaiten is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If the game starts to use information in a new way, this page is updated first, and the
date at the top changes.
